## 🚀 About Me & My Journey

I am a **Computer Science undergraduate** with a strong passion for **investigating systems, debugging complex issues, and automating processes**. I enjoy understanding how software works under the hood rather than just learning how to use it.

During my **2 years of corporate IT experience**, I worked extensively with infrastructure and operational routines, where I independently developed an **internal Java application** that automated technical reporting, reducing manual processing time by 10 minutes per ticket.

My academic and independent research heavily focuses on **AI and Machine Learning**. I have successfully replicated research papers, including:
- **Computer Vision:** Replicated an Oxford research paper for automated penguin counting, comparing traditional ML (SVM + HOG) against Deep Learning (a modified VGG-16 CNN architecture).
- **Time-Series Forecasting:** Developed energy consumption prediction models using **LSTM neural networks**, dealing directly with memory states and vanishing gradient mitigations.

Beyond academia, I am an active **Open Source contributor**. I frequently engage in bug investigation, log analysis, and proposing fixes for projects I use daily, such as **Microsoft Terminal**, **Brave Browser**, and the **HyDE Project**.
